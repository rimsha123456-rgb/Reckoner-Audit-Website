About Us

Find answers to the questions we’re most frequently asked about Reckoner Audit, our services and our approach. This section provides clear and useful information to help you better understand what we do, how we work and what you can expect when working with our team.

We offer several types of audits, including:
External Audits – independent reviews of financial statements for stakeholders.
Internal Audits – reviews to assess internal controls and risk management.
Compliance Audits – ensuring adherence to laws and regulations.
Forensic Audits – investigating financial discrepancies or fraud.

Timelines depend on size, complexity, readiness and reporting deadlines.

Fees are scoped according to the entity, risk profile and reporting requirements.
